# 🎯 Complete Navigation Guide - ResearchNet

## **After Successful Sign-In: Where to Click Next**

### **🚀 Step 1: You'll Land on Dashboard**
**URL:** `http://localhost:5173/dashboard`

**What You'll See:**
- Welcome message: "Welcome back, [Your Name]!"
- 4 Statistics cards (Papers, Reviews, Connections, Citations)
- 2 Quick Action buttons
- Recent Activity section

---

## **🎯 Main Navigation - Top Bar**

### **Always Visible Navigation:**
```
[ResearchNet Logo] [Dashboard] [Feed] [Papers] [Network] [Publish] [Profile] [Logout]
```

**Click Anywhere to Navigate:**

### **1. 📊 Dashboard** (Main Hub)
**Click:** "Dashboard" in top bar or "ResearchNet" logo
**What You Get:**
- Overview of your research activity
- Quick access to all features
- Statistics and recent activity

### **2. 📱 Social Feed** (See Others' Activity)
**Click:** "Feed" in top bar
**What You Get:**
- Posts and papers from your network
- Like, comment, and share content
- Filter by All Activity, Papers, or Posts

### **3. 📚 Browse Papers** (Discover Research)
**Click:** "Papers" in top bar
**What You Get:**
- Search and browse research papers
- Filter by category and sort options
- Click any paper to view details

### **4. 👥 Network** (Connect with Researchers)
**Click:** "Network" in top bar
**What You Get:**
- View your connections
- See suggested researchers
- Connect/disconnect with researchers

### **5. 📝 Publish** (Share Your Research)
**Click:** "Publish" in top bar
**What You Get:**
- Comprehensive paper submission form
- Add authors, methodology, results
- Upload PDF and submit for review

### **6. 👤 Profile** (Manage Your Info)
**Click:** "Profile" in top bar
**What You Get:**
- Update personal information
- Add professional details
- Manage research interests

---

## **🎯 Quick Actions from Dashboard**

### **Dashboard Cards - Click These:**

#### **Card 1: "Publish New Research"**
**Click:** "Start Publishing" button
**Takes You To:** Paper publishing form
**What You Can Do:**
- Fill comprehensive paper details
- Add multiple authors
- Upload PDF file
- Submit for review

#### **Card 2: "Browse Research"**
**Click:** "Browse Papers" button
**Takes You To:** Papers browsing page
**What You Can Do:**
- Search papers by title/author
- Filter by category
- Sort by date/citations
- Click papers to view details

## **🎯 Detailed Feature Access**

### **📱 Social Feed Features**
**Access:** Click "Feed" in top bar

**What You Can Do:**
1. **View Activity:** See posts and papers from your network
2. **Filter Content:** Use tabs (All Activity, Papers, Posts)
3. **Interact:** Like, comment, share posts
4. **Create Posts:** Click "Share Update" button

**Interactive Elements:**
- ❤️ **Like Button:** Click heart to like posts/papers
- 💬 **Comment Button:** Click to add comments
- 🔄 **Share Button:** Click to share content
- 📝 **Share Update:** Click to create new posts

### **📚 Papers Features**
**Access:** Click "Papers" in top bar

**What You Can Do:**
1. **Search:** Use search bar to find specific papers
2. **Filter:** Select category (Computer Science, Physics, etc.)
3. **Sort:** Choose sorting (Date, Citations, Downloads, Title)
4. **View Details:** Click any paper card

**Paper Card Actions:**
- **Click Paper Card:** Opens detailed paper view
- **View Statistics:** See citations, downloads
- **Read Abstract:** Preview paper content

### **📄 Paper Details Features**
**Access:** Click any paper card from Papers page

**What You Can Do:**
1. **Read Full Paper:** Complete content with all sections
2. **View Author Info:** See author profiles and affiliations
3. **Interact:** Like, bookmark, share, comment
4. **Download:** Get PDF (simulated)
5. **Cite:** Generate citations

**Interactive Elements:**
- ❤️ **Like:** Click heart to like paper
- 🔖 **Bookmark:** Click to save for later
- 🔄 **Share:** Click to share paper
- 💬 **Comment:** Add your thoughts
- 📥 **Download:** Get PDF file
- 📋 **Cite:** Generate citation

### **👥 Network Features**
**Access:** Click "Network" in top bar

**What You Can Do:**
1. **View Connections:** See your current network
2. **See Suggestions:** Discover new researchers
3. **Connect:** Click "Connect" to add researchers
4. **View Profiles:** Click "View Profile" for details
5. **Manage:** Disconnect if needed

**Interactive Elements:**
- 🔗 **Connect Button:** Add researchers to network
- 👁️ **View Profile:** See detailed researcher info
- ❌ **Disconnect:** Remove from network
- 📊 **Statistics:** Track network growth

### **✍️ Create Posts Features**
**Access:** Click "Share Update" from Feed page

**What You Can Do:**
1. **Choose Post Type:** General, Paper Share, Collaboration
2. **Write Content:** Share your thoughts (up to 1000 chars)
3. **Add Tags:** Help others discover your post
4. **Set Visibility:** Public, Connections Only, Private
5. **Attach Files:** Upload documents/images

**Post Types:**
- 📝 **General Post:** Share thoughts and updates
- 📄 **Paper Share:** Discuss research papers
- 🤝 **Collaboration:** Request research partners

### **📝 Publish Papers Features**
**Access:** Click "Publish" in top bar

**What You Can Do:**
1. **Basic Info:** Title, abstract, keywords
2. **Authors:** Add multiple authors with affiliations
3. **Research Content:** Methodology, results, conclusion
4. **Additional Info:** References, funding, acknowledgments
5. **File Upload:** Submit PDF document

**Form Sections:**
- 📋 **Basic Information:** Title, abstract, keywords
- 👥 **Authors:** Multiple author management
- 🔬 **Research Content:** Methodology, results, conclusion
- 📚 **Additional Info:** References, funding, acknowledgments
- 📄 **File Upload:** PDF submission

### **👤 Profile Features**
**Access:** Click "Profile" in top bar

**What You Can Do:**
1. **Personal Info:** Name, email, phone, affiliation
2. **Professional Details:** Specialization, bio, interests
3. **Online Presence:** Website, ORCID, Google Scholar
4. **Education:** Academic background
5. **Experience:** Professional history

**Profile Sections:**
- 👤 **Personal Information:** Basic details
- 🎓 **Professional Details:** Research info
- 🌐 **Online Presence:** Social links
- 📚 **Education & Experience:** Background

## **🎯 User Experience Flow**

5. **Click "Share Update"** → Post about your work
6. **Click "Publish"** → Share new findings
