## **🎯 Main Navigation - Top Bar (Always Visible)**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [ResearchNet] [Dashboard] [Feed] [Papers] [Network] [Publish] [Profile] [Logout] │

```

**Click Any Button to Navigate:**

### **📊 Dashboard** (Main Hub)
- **Click:** "Dashboard" or "ResearchNet" logo
- **What You Get:** Overview, statistics, quick actions, getting started guide

### **📱 Feed** (Social Activity)
- **Click:** "Feed" button
- **What You Get:** Posts and papers from your network, like/comment/share

### **📚 Papers** (Research Discovery)
- **Click:** "Papers" button
- **What You Get:** Browse, search, filter research papers

### **👥 Network** (Connection Management)
- **Click:** "Network" button
- **What You Get:** Connect with researchers, manage your network

### **📝 Publish** (Research Sharing)
- **Click:** "Publish" button
- **What You Get:** Submit papers for publication

### **👤 Profile** (Personal Management)
- **Click:** "Profile" button
- **What You Get:** Update your information and research interests

## **🎯 Complete User Journey**

### **New User Flow (Step by Step):**

```
1. Sign In → Dashboard
   ↓
2. Click "Go to Profile" → Complete Profile
   ↓
3. Click "Browse Papers" → Discover Research
   ↓
4. Click any paper → View Details
   ↓
5. Click "Manage Network" → Connect with Researchers
   ↓
6. Click "View Feed" → See Social Activity
   ↓
7. Click "Start Publishing" → Share Your Research
```

### **Daily Usage Flow:**

```
1. Click "Feed" → Check Latest Activity
   ↓
2. Click "Papers" → Read New Research
   ↓
3. Click paper cards → View Details & Comment
   ↓
4. Click "Network" → Manage Connections
   ↓
5. Click "Share Update" → Post About Your Work
   ↓
6. Click "Publish" → Share New Findings
```
